I work on post-training and reinforcement learning for foundation models at Xiaohongshu, with a focus on Search agents and Cowork agents.

I received my M.S. in Software Engineering from Peking University, advised by Prof. Weiping Li. My research focuses on reliable reasoning, long-horizon agent execution in complex environments, and expert-level synthetic environment generation. During my graduate studies, I published eight papers at top-tier conferences as first or co-first author, including ACL, CVPR, and WWW.

我目前就职于小红书 Pevek 基础模型团队，主要研究基础模型的后训练与强化学习，重点关注 Search Agent 与 Cowork Agent。

我硕士毕业于北京大学软件工程专业，师从李伟平教授。我的研究聚焦于如何提升模型推理的可靠性，让 Agent 能够在更复杂的环境中成功完成长程任务，并探索如何构建媲美人类专家水平的高质量合成环境。在研究生期间，我以第一作者/共同一作发表了8篇顶会文章，包括ACL、CVPR、WWW等。
